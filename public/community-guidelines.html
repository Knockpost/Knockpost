Community Guidelines

Last Updated: May 24, 2026
Version: 1.0

Knockpost aims to maintain a safe, respectful, and authentic platform for all users.

By using Knockpost, users agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Respect Others

Users must treat others respectfully.

The following behaviors are prohibited:

  • Harassment
  • Bullying
  • Threats
  • Intimidation
  • Hate speech
  • Discrimination
  • Abusive behavior

2. Authenticity

Users must not:

  • Impersonate other individuals or organizations
  • Create fake accounts for harmful purposes
  • Spread scams, misinformation, or deceptive posts, Waves, Glimpse, and uploaded content

3. Spam & Manipulation

Users may not:

  • Mass spam posts or messages
  • Artificially increase engagement
  • Use bots for abusive activity
  • Manipulate platform systems unfairly

4. Harmful Content

Do not upload or share:

  • Malware
  • Dangerous links
  • Illegal material
  • Violent or exploitative content
  • Content promoting self-harm or criminal activity

5. Privacy & Safety

Users should respect the privacy and safety of others.

Sharing private or confidential information without permission is prohibited.

6. Illegal Activity

Knockpost may not be used for unlawful activities including:

  • Fraud
  • Identity theft
  • Illegal transactions
  • Copyright violations
  • Unauthorized access attempts

7. Content Enforcement

Knockpost reserves the right to:

  • Remove violating posts, Waves, Glimpse, and uploaded content
  • Limit visibility of posts, Waves, Glimpse, and uploaded content
  • Restrict account functionality
  • Suspend or permanently terminate accounts

8. Repeat Violations

Repeated or severe violations may result in permanent removal from the platform.

9. Guideline Updates

These Community Guidelines may be updated periodically.

Continued use of Knockpost means acceptance of updated guidelines.
